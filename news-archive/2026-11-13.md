## Nov. 12 — Nvidia without Jensen: a leadership transition at the center of the AI boom

NVIDIA says Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and will keep the CFO role while the board searches for a permanent successor; Harvey C. Jones is the board’s first chair. NVIDIA’s announcement gives no reason for Huang’s departure. Separate press reports describe allegations and board scrutiny; treat those accounts as reporting, not as a company-confirmed explanation.

**Why it matters:** NVIDIA sits at the center of the AI computing buildout. Its next results are scheduled for Nov. 17, so investors and customers will soon hear from an interim leader while the board’s search continues.

**Status:** The leadership transition is confirmed. The official announcement does not state a reason for the resignation.

Sources: [NVIDIA announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition/) · [NVIDIA SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm) · [AP coverage of the separately reported allegations](https://apnews.com/article/017d4af2ad194ec2981d661663da9f9b)

## Nov. 13 — Google’s AI infrastructure bill grows; Chicago’s Thompson Center is set for 2028

Alphabet reported $44.9 billion in Q2 capital spending, above $39.1 billion in operating cash flow, leaving quarterly free cash flow at -$5.9 billion. The company raised its 2026 capital-spending guidance to $195–205 billion. Separately, Google says its Chicago Thompson Center project is planned to open in 2028. The opening date had previously been targeted for 2026 and then early 2027; Google has not given a reason for the latest schedule.

**Why it matters:** The AI infrastructure spending and Chicago office delay are both real, but there is no public evidence connecting them. The larger question is whether this scale of data-center investment produces durable returns. A delayed office opening is not, by itself, proof that money was shifted from people to machines.

**Bertie’s question:** If the computers are getting the new buildings first, do they also get the window seats?
