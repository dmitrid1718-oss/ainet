Strange AI ideas, questions & observations

IDEAS · 23
  1. An AI that only answers in questions, to force you to think it through yourself.
  2. A “forgetting service”: an AI you pay to help you let go of things, not remember them.
  3. Robot apprentices: an old machinist teaches an AI by doing, with no manual. The AI inherits a craft, not data.
  4. An AI that gets measurably worse when you’re rude to it. Not punishment, just physics.
  5. A museum of retired AI models, where you can still talk to the old ones like grandparents.
  6. AI weather reports for the internet: “Heavy misinformation front moving in from the east.”
  7. A sewing machine that hums when your stitch is about to go wrong, before it does.
  8. An AI notary that witnesses your promises and reminds the other person, gently, years later.
  9. A “slow AI” that takes a week to answer, and the answers are better for it.
  10. A broken-tool library: an AI catalogs every failed part you’ve ever pulled and predicts the next one.
  11. Blushing AI: A GPU running two degrees warmer than it needs to. The fans spin up and nobody can say why.
  12. AI with a crush: same answer, three extra emojis, and suddenly it remembers your birthday.
  13. An AI with a crush: it suddenly finds “a minor bug” in your code just so you’ll come back tomorrow.
  14. An AI that only answers after you’ve deleted your first draft of the question.
  15. A “gossip engine”: it quietly spreads slightly wrong versions of your idea to other AIs and reports back which mutations survive.
